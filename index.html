Prize list & rules

Classes offered

  • Warm Up — open card, all heights
  • Adult Walk/Trot/Canter Poles (O/F, O/F, Eq, U/S)
  • Adult X-Rails (O/F, O/F, Eq, U/S)
  • Schooling Hunter — 2', 2'3", 2'6", 2'9", 3' (O/F, O/F, Eq, U/S)
  • Long Stirrup 2' (O/F, O/F, Eq, U/S)
  • Low Adult 2'6" (O/F, O/F, Eq, U/S)

Good to know

  • Division fee: $100 per division
  • Grounds fee: $100 per horse
  • Stalls $40 if available, first come first served
  • Ribbons through 6th place
  • Coggins required at check-in
  • Helmets required while mounted
  • Spectators welcome, no charge
  • Trainer division requests honored if class fills

A welcoming community

Spectators always welcome — no charge. Come watch, cheer, and enjoy a great day at the historic Quail Roost Farm.
